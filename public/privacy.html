Privacy policy

LetterScan. Last updated: 19 October 2026.

Short version: LetterScan has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no trackers. Photos and the text read from them are processed and stored only on your phone. Nothing is sent to us. The only exception is the optional AI mode, which you have to switch on yourself and which needs your own xAI API key (see below).

What the app does with your data

  • Photos of documents. When you take or choose a photo, the app reads the text on your phone (on-device text recognition). The photo is not uploaded anywhere.
  • Cards you save. Saved cards (the extracted fields, the text read from the photo, a small preview and the photos of the pages) are stored in the app's own storage on your phone (IndexedDB). They stay there until you delete them (Saved list or Settings → "Delete all saved cards"), or until you delete the app. In the web version, the browser may also clear this storage if the site is not installed and space runs low.
  • The card that is open. To survive an app update or a reload, the currently open card is kept temporarily in the app's session storage on your phone. It is removed when the session ends.
  • Settings. Your language choices, whether AI mode is on, and your xAI API key (if you enter one) are stored on your phone in the app's local storage.
  • Reminders. In the web version, "Remind me" creates a calendar file (.ics) that you can add to your own calendar. In the iPhone app, it schedules local notifications on your phone (3 days before the deadline and on the day). No server is involved.
  • Sharing and PDF export. These only happen when you tap Share or PDF, and only go where you choose in your phone's share sheet.
  • Backup files. Where available, "Back up all cards" creates one file with your saved cards and settings (never your API key). It is saved only where you choose (for example Files or iCloud Drive). Restoring reads a file you pick and adds the cards to your phone; nothing is sent to us.
  • Photo check. Before reading, the app checks on your phone whether a photo is too dark, blurred, tilted or cut off, and may suggest taking it again. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Read aloud and Easy Language. "Read aloud" uses your phone's built-in text-to-speech voices; the text stays on your phone. Easy Language is made by the app on your phone from the card.
  • Payment QR codes. The app can read a payment QR code on a letter (EPC/GiroCode, Swiss QR-bill) and can make one for your banking app. Both happen on your phone. The app never makes a payment; it shows the details so you can check them before you pay in your own banking app.
  • Date you received a letter. If you enter the date a letter arrived, it is used on your phone to work out the deadline and saved with the card.
  • Reading languages. Document languages are read on your phone. The iPhone app ships with all language data. In the web version, the language data for some reading languages is downloaded once from the same site that hosts the app (GitHub Pages) the first time you use that language, then kept on your device.
  • Purchases. If LetterScan offers an optional in-app purchase, it is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never receive your card or payment details.

Optional AI mode (off by default)

AI mode is never on unless you choose it: switching it on in Settings first shows a consent screen that explains what is sent and to whom, and nothing is sent unless you tap "Agree and turn on". If you switch on AI mode and enter your own xAI API key, then, when you are online, the text read from a document (never the photo), today's date and your chosen language are sent directly from your phone to xAI (api.x.ai) to help fill in the card. Before anything is sent, the app hides personal data it can recognise on your phone (names, addresses, IBANs and account numbers, tax IDs, phone numbers, email addresses, dates of birth and file or reference numbers) by replacing them with placeholders such as [IBAN_1], and shows you the exact text that will be sent. Nothing is sent unless you tap Send; if you cancel, the card is made offline. The real values never leave your phone and are put back into the card on your phone. Automatic hiding can miss something, so check the preview before you send. This request is made with your key, under your own agreement with xAI; xAI's own privacy policy applies to it: x.ai/legal/privacy-policy. We do not receive or see this data. Turn AI mode off or remove your key in Settings at any time.

What we collect

Nothing. We (the developer) do not run a server for this app and do not receive your photos, documents, cards, settings or usage data. The app contains no analytics or advertising SDKs.

The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address when your browser downloads the app files; see GitHub's privacy statement: GitHub General Privacy Statement. The iPhone app ships with all its files and does not contact GitHub.

Permissions (iPhone app)

  • Camera: to photograph a document. Only used when you tap "Take a photo".
  • Photos: to open a photo or screenshot you pick. The app only sees the photo you choose.
  • Notifications: only if you tap "Remind me", for deadline reminders.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any personal data from anyone.

Deleting your data

Delete single cards in the Saved list, or everything in Settings → "Delete all saved cards". Deleting the app removes all its data from your phone.
