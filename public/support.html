Help & contact

Action Card helps you understand official paperwork in a language you can't fully read: parking and traffic fines, rental and move-in sheets, hotel and airline cancellations, visa and residence letters, and medicine labels. It works in English, German, French, Spanish, Italian and Portuguese.

Contact

Email: SUPPORT_EMAIL_PLACEHOLDER. Please don't send photos of personal documents; describe the problem instead.

Important

The app can be wrong. It reads photos with on-device text recognition and simple rules. Always check every date and amount against the original document; each value shows the exact text it was taken from. Action Card is not legal, financial or medical advice. For medicine, always follow the label and ask a pharmacist or doctor.

Common questions

Does it need the internet? No. Everything works offline. Only the optional AI mode uses the internet.

Where are my cards? Only on your phone. Deleting the app deletes them. Export a PDF if you want a copy.

The result is wrong or empty. Retake the photo closer, flat and in good light, with the whole page in view. You can also edit any value by tapping it, or add more pages to the same card.

Reminders don't appear. Allow notifications for Action Card in iPhone Settings → Notifications, then tap "Remind me" again.

What is AI mode? An optional setting for people who have their own xAI API key. It is off by default. See the privacy policy.
