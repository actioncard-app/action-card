Help & contact

LetterScan helps you understand official paperwork in a language you can't fully read: parking and traffic fines, rental and move-in sheets, hotel and airline cancellations, visa and residence letters, and medicine labels. It works in English, German, French, Spanish, Italian and Portuguese.

Contact

Email: letterscanhelp@gmail.com. Please don't send photos of personal documents; describe the problem instead.

Important

The app can be wrong. It reads photos with on-device text recognition and simple rules. Always check every date and amount against the original document; each value shows the exact text it was taken from. LetterScan is not legal, financial or medical advice. For medicine, always follow the label and ask a pharmacist or doctor.

Common questions

Does it need the internet? No. Everything works offline.

Where are my cards? Only on your phone. Deleting the app deletes them. Export a PDF if you want a copy.

The result is wrong or empty. Retake the photo closer, flat and in good light, with the whole page in view. You can also edit any value by tapping it, or add more pages to the same card.

Reminders don't appear. Allow notifications for LetterScan in iPhone Settings → Notifications, then tap "Remind me" again.

What is LetterScan Pro? An optional subscription in the iPhone app, monthly or yearly ($7.99 per month or $39.99 per year in the US; your local price is shown in the app), with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers. The free version gives you 4 scans every 30 days (the sample letter doesn't count; the 30 days start with your first scan, and the app shows how many are left and the reset date before you scan), the basic card with deadline, amount and the one next step, read aloud with the iPhone voice, 1 saved letter, one default reminder and all languages. The scan count is kept only on your iPhone, so reinstalling the app resets it. Pro adds unlimited scans and multi-page letters, the full step-by-step plan and reply templates, natural voices, unlimited saved letters with backup and restore, PDF export, sharing and calendar, your own reminder times, payment QR reading and colour themes. When the trial ends, the subscription renews automatically each month until you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or the current period ends. Cancel or manage it any time in iPhone Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions, or at apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions. Payment and refunds are handled by Apple. Terms of Use (EULA).

Is there an AI mode? No. LetterScan reads your documents entirely on your device, in the iPhone app and on the web. See the privacy policy.
